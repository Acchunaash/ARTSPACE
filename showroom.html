A considered space

Designed to make the pieces feel at home.

The showroom is built around a curved limestone pavilion, a bronze-framed glass entrance and a warm, gallery-like interior. Move around the model to explore its form and the relationship between the exterior and interior.

Inside the first fit-out: display islands, sculptural seating, hanging rails, mirrors, planters and layered architectural lighting. These are spatial design elements, not finished product displays.
